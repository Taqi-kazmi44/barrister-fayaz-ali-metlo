Practice Areas

Twelve areas of practice before the Sindh High Court and the courts of Sindh, each supported by reported judgments and a continuing caseload.

01

Constitutional Law

Constitutional petitions before the Sindh High Court under Article 199, fundamental rights, and challenges to state action.

02

Banking & Finance

Banking litigation, finance disputes, recovery matters and regulatory questions for banks and financial institutions.

03

Corporate & Commercial

Company disputes, shareholder matters and commercial litigation under the Companies Act 2017.

04

Criminal Litigation

Trial and appellate criminal defence, bail, quashment and white collar matters.

05

Civil Litigation

Suits, injunctions, appeals and execution proceedings across the civil courts of Sindh.

06

Property & Land Revenue

Title disputes, amenity plots, possession, and land revenue matters.

07

Family Law

Khula, maintenance, custody and inheritance matters handled with discretion.

08

Customs & Taxation

09

Civil service, promotion, and master and servant disputes.

11

Admiralty & Insurance

Admiralty suits and appeals, cargo claims, and insurance litigation.

12

Arbitration & Mediation

Domestic arbitration and negotiated settlement of commercial disputes.

Services

How Chambers Works

Advocacy

Appearances before the Sindh High Court, district courts and tribunals, in petitions, suits, appeals and references.

Advisory

Written opinions on questions of Pakistani law for companies, banks and individuals, before a dispute becomes litigation.

Drafting

Pleadings, constitutional petitions, contracts and commercial documents drafted with an accountant's eye for figures.

Arbitration & Mediation

Representation in domestic arbitration and structured negotiation where a settlement serves the client better than a decree.
