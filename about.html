Profile

A Barrister Built on
Merit and Preparation

Barrister Fayaz Ali Metlo is a Partner at Qaiser & Metlo Law Chambers, Karachi, a set of consultants and advocates providing consultancy, advocacy, arbitration and mediation services, whose members have been involved in some of the leading cases for over 40 years.

He earned his LL.M as a Gold Medalist and brings an accounting background to his practice, a combination that makes him particularly effective in banking, corporate, customs and taxation disputes where the numbers decide the case.

He appears regularly before the Sindh High Court in constitutional petitions, banking and civil matters, and his chamber team supports litigation across District and Sessions Courts, Accountability Courts, Banking Courts, special courts and tribunals.

Within the legal fraternity, he is known for mentoring young advocates and for his work for the bar. He is a candidate for General Secretary of the Karachi Bar Association (Elections 2026–27), running on experience, dedication and integrity.
