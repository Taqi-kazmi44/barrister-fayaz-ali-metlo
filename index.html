FAQ

Common Questions

Quick answers before you book.

Which courts does Barrister Metlo practise in?

Primarily the Sindh High Court at Karachi, along with District and Sessions Courts, Banking Courts, Accountability Courts, special courts and tribunals across Sindh.

How do I book a consultation?

Use the contact form on this website with a short summary of your matter. The chambers will respond with an appointment time and a list of documents to bring.

What should I bring to the first meeting?

Bring your CNIC, all notices, orders or agreements connected with the matter, and a one-page timeline of events. Originals are only needed at the filing stage.

Does the chambers handle matters outside Karachi?

Yes. Through Qaiser and Metlo Law Chambers, matters are coordinated across Sindh, and appellate strategy is planned from the Karachi office.
