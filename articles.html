Short, plain notes on reported judgments from the practice, written for clients, students and fellow members of the Bar. Each note is drawn from the reported judgment itself, which is linked in full.

Case Note · Company Law · 2025 CLD 1814

When a Subsidiary Holds Shares in Its Own Holding Company

In M. Ziaullah Khan Chishti v. TRG Pakistan Limited, the Sindh High Court examined sections 86(2) and 286 of the Companies Act 2017 and the doctrine of a continuing wrong: a wrong that repeats itself is not defeated by limitation in the ordinary way. Barrister Metlo appeared as counsel in the case.

Case Note · Taxation · 2025 PTD 1777

Royalty and Petroleum Concessions in an Income Tax Reference

Occidental Petroleum (Pakistan) Inc. v. Commissioner Inland Revenue raised, by way of a tax reference, how royalty payments under petroleum concessions are treated for income tax. The judgment is a useful map of how references under the Income Tax Ordinance are framed and answered.

Case Note · Constitutional · PLD 2025 Sindh 138

Who May File a Constitutional Petition? Locus Standi under Article 199

Not every grievance opens the doors of Article 199. In Salahuddin v. Province of Sindh, the Court considered the standing a petitioner must show before the High Court will entertain a constitutional petition. Barrister Metlo appeared for the petitioner.

Case Note · Taxation · 2024 PTD 290

Exemptions on Pharmaceutical Packing Material

Getz Pharma (Pvt) Limited v. Federation of Pakistan concerned sales tax and customs exemption on material used to pack medicines, a question that turns entirely on the exact words of the exemption notifications. Barrister Metlo appeared for the appellants.
